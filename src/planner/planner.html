Plan layout

Each card is a full-screen capture window. Split or close panes with their buttons and drag the gaps to resize. Drag a pane onto another pane of the same window: drop on its middle to swap them, or near an edge to place it on that side. Each pane's menu picks a shell, a ptmux, tmux or screen session, a linuxserver app such as a webtop desktop or Chromium (run in Docker), the screen of a connected Android device (through scrcpy), or a web page; a session or app keeps running when its pane closes, so any pane in any window can pick it up. Changes apply as you make them.
